Magán, Martin Sasieta, and Alejandro Vilar López The authors refine and extend the statistical interpretation of the black hole entropy formula to wide classes of black holes such as rotating and charged black holes, including extremal and near-extremal solutions, with or without supersymmetry. They achieve this by constructing a set of black hole microstates with semiclassical interiors that span a Hilbert space of dimension eS, where S is the black hole entropy. [Phys. Rev.
