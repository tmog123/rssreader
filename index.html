A Kitaev nematic phase emerges from a delicate biquadratic-dimer background through an Ising quantum critical point, which further evolves into a Kitaev dimer phase that spontaneously breaks screw symmetry and crystallizes into a gapped order of alternating ℤ2 bond parities.
