    Analysis of real-space transport channels for electrons and holes in halide perovskites
    Author(s): Frederik Vonhoff, Maximilian J. Schilcher, David R. Reichman, and David A. Egger Carrier transport in halide perovskite semiconductors is difficult to predict computationally because these systems show unusual vibrational and electron-phonon coupling mechanism. The authors used a dynamic disorder model that was parameterized from first-principles to analyze temporal orbital occupations and investigate how material-specific on-site energy levels and spin-orbit coupling strengths influence carrier mobilities. They find that both on-site energy gaps and halide spin-orbit coupling significantly affect carrier dynamics driven by three transport channels, which helps explain the differing behaviors of electron and hole mobility across different halide perovskites.
