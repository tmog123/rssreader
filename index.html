    On-chip direct-current source for scalable superconducting quantum computing
    Author(s): Lei Jiang et al. Applying magnetic flux to manipulate qubits is an important method in superconducting quantum computing, but the state-of-the-art approach based on room-temperature electronics suffers from some unscalable limitations. This work provides an alternative approach in which an rf SQUID serves as an on-chip source of direct current, to provide qubits with in situ, low-noise magnetic flux. Several single-pulse inputs are enough to modulate the source and provide qubits with flux; this can benefit from time-division multiplexing to save on cables and sources. The technique could provide a scalable solution for applying magnetic flux in fault-tolerant quantum computing. [Phys. Rev. Applied 24, 034057] Published Mon Sep 22, 2025
