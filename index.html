They observed step-like transitions and identified the orthogonality point of the rare-earth and iron sublattices. Ultrasound-velocity measurements, performed up to 85 T, confirmed one of the transitions. Based on these results, the authors determined the intersublattice exchange field, establishing Dy₂Fe₁₇ as a model system for extreme-field magnetism. [Phys. Rev.
