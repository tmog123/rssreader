The authors explore here an antiferromagnetic ordered structure comprising one-dimensional Nd chains connected via the triangular lattice in Nd3Rh4Sn13, which is superimposed on the chiral structure like that in several Remeika phase compounds. The results suggest that simultaneously broken spatial and time-reversal symmetries in this material open an avenue to investigate magnetic interactions mediated by the topological electrons protected by the noncentrosymmetric chiral structure.
