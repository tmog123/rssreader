    Author(s): Ajay K. Poonia, Naresh Aggarwal, Swapnil S. Deshpande, Barnali Mondal, Angshuman Nag, Sudip Chakraborty, and K. V. Here, the authors demonstrate that intrinsic positional disorder in a metal halide perovskite quantum dot solid usually treated as a defect of such assemblies, can instead strongly suppress hot-carrier cooling. Ultrafast transient absorption spectroscopy reveals hot carriers localized in continuum states, with cooling times more than an order of magnitude longer than those in uncoupled quantum dots.
